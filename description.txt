Time in Zone — a single data field that accumulates time spent in a heart-rate zone.

Two modes:
- Current Zone (live): shows time in the zone you're in right now, switching as your HR moves.
- Target Zone: tracks one zone (e.g. Z2) so you can see whether you banked your base minutes.

Zones are read from your device's configured HR zones (Max HR / %Max) — nothing hardcoded.
